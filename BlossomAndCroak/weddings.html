A La Carte Floral

Weddings & Events

Celebrate your special day with fresh, seasonal flowers grown right here on our farm. We offer a relaxed, à la carte approach to wedding florals that is perfect for couples who appreciate the natural beauty of locally grown blooms without the need for a full-service floral package.

Our offerings include:

  • Bridal bouquets
  • Bridesmaids' bouquets
  • Boutonnieres
  • Centerpieces & table arrangements
  • Ceremony florals
  • DIY bulk flower buckets

A limited number of 2026 dates are still available, and we are now booking for 2027!

We would love to hear all about your day! Please feel free to get in touch!

Proudly serving couples across Northumberland County and beyond, including Warkworth, Campbellford, Brighton, Cobourg, Port Hope, Trenton, Belleville and the GTA.
